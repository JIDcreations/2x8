Software

Not every business needs custom software. Often the package already exists and the problem is in the choice, the setup or the connection. That is where we help, without selling you something we built ourselves.

What you get

  • We bring you expertise across a wide range of software solutions
  • We help you integrate and configure them
  • Ideal if you don’t need custom software, but do need help choosing
  • Concretely: Odoo, Notion, and your mail set up through Google Workspace or Microsoft 365

What that looks like

  • A till and an online shop selling from the same stock
  • Quotes, invoices and customers in one system instead of four
  • A migration where not a single old customer is left behind

Frequently asked questions

Which packages do you work with?

Odoo, Notion, Softtouch and your mail environment through Google Workspace or Microsoft 365, among others. What you already use counts for as much as what you could be using.

Do you sell licences?

No. We advise, set up and connect. You buy the licence directly, so you are not tied to us.

Can existing data come along?

That is usually the nerviest part of a migration, and exactly what we watch: customers, invoices and history have to be complete on the other side.

When is custom software the better call?

When no package does what you do, or when you have to bend one so far that every update hurts. Then we would rather build — see platforms.
