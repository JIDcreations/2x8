Which CMS?

The system you keep your own site up to date with. Three we meet often, laid out side by side.

What is a CMS?

A content management system is the back of your site: the place where you change the text yourself, swap out photos and add pages, without a line of code involved. Which one suits you best depends on what you sell, how much you want to keep in your own hands, and how far you want to be able to go later on.

Three platforms.

WordPress

The most used system in the world, and the most stretchable.

Strong
  • From a simple site to a web shop or a member portal: it all fits
  • Everything sits on your own hosting, so you’re tied to nobody
  • Full control over speed, structure and findability
Watch out
  • A few more buttons to get to know
  • Updates need following up — that’s on us

Pick this if your site has to grow with you.

Shopify

A platform that does one thing: sell online, and does it very well.

Strong
  • Payments, shipping, stock and till come as standard
  • Hosting, security and updates aren’t your problem
  • A large app store for anything else you need
Watch out
  • A monthly subscription, plus a cost per sale
  • As an ordinary site, outside the shop, it’s less suitable

Pick this if selling is the heart of your business.

Wix

Drag and drop. Something online fast, without much explaining.

Strong
  • You’ll have something standing in an afternoon
  • One fixed price a month, all in
  • No technical upkeep on your side
Watch out
  • You stay inside what Wix offers; going further isn’t possible
  • Moving to another system means starting over

Pick this if it can stay small and has to be quick.

What we recommend

We’d rather work with WordPress or a site built from scratch: everything stays yours and there’s room to add later. If your business runs on selling, Shopify is often the shortest route. And if it can stay small and temporary, we’ll say that too. We don’t choose for you — we explain what each road means, and we decide together.
