Keikoku Atelier

A quiet, image-led site for an architecture practice that designs houses meant to last a hundred years.

Client
Keikoku Atelier
Year
2026
Services
Design, Development
Online
Visit site ↗
Code
GitHub ↗

Challenge

22 private houses and headquarters across three continents, designed by a studio that swears by “subtraction”: removing windows, walls and gestures until only the necessary structure remains. That restraint had to come through in the site itself, not just in the words about it.

Approach

Large photography, minimal typography, and four flagship commissions told in as much image as text: Residence Ao in Hakone, The Kestrel House on Ibiza, Vantor Headquarters in Zurich, Villa Noon in Marrakech. Coordinates as a quiet signature rather than decoration.

Result

A site that applies “subtraction” itself: the practice's design principle, translated into the medium of the screen.
